Overview

Sri Krishna Spiral CT, 3D Dental CBCT & MRI Scan Centre is a diagnostic imaging centre in Secunderabad. The website brings together information about diagnostic services, advanced imaging technology, patient-focused care and appointment enquiries in one digital experience.

UI/UX Designer

The Challenge

Healthcare websites often contain a large amount of information, but patients usually come to them with a very simple goal.

Understand the service→Decide what they need→Book or enquire

The challenge was to create a trustworthy and easy-to-navigate experience that presents complex diagnostic services without overwhelming patients.

Core User Journey

A direct path from discovery to booking

Discover→ Explore Services→ Understand the Scan→ Build Trust→ Book Appointment

The current site places Book Appointment and Explore Services prominently in the hero, followed by service information, diagnostic benefits, patient-focused content and supporting information.

Key UX Areas
01

Service Discovery

Structured diagnostic services so visitors can quickly understand what the centre offers.

Spiral CT · 3D Dental CBCT · ECG · MRI-related information
02

Building Patient Trust

Healthcare decisions require confidence. The experience uses the centre's history, expertise, technology and patient-focused messaging to establish credibility.

25+ years of diagnostic experience · 10,000+ happy patients
03

Appointment Access

The primary actions are surfaced early so users can move from discovering a service to taking action without navigating through unnecessary pages.

04

Explaining Complex Services

Diagnostic imaging can be difficult for non-medical users to understand. The content structure separates services and explains their purpose in simpler sections.

05

Responsive Experience

The interface was designed to maintain clear hierarchy and accessible actions across desktop and smaller screens.

Design Focus

Clarity

Make healthcare information easier to scan and understand.

Trust

Use visual hierarchy and content structure to communicate expertise and credibility.

Accessibility

Keep important information and actions easy to find.

Conversion

Create a clear path from service discovery to appointment enquiry.

Key Sections

Hero

Introduces the centre's diagnostic capabilities while immediately presenting the primary appointment action.

About

Communicates the centre's history, experience and approach to diagnostic care.

Services

Presents the main diagnostic services and gives users a clear starting point for exploring them.

Why It Matters

Explains why accurate imaging is important for diagnosis and treatment planning, particularly across dental and craniofacial care.

Insights

Provides an editorial space for healthcare information, patient stories and educational content.

Outcome

Designed a structured healthcare website experience that makes diagnostic services easier to discover, builds trust through clear information and creates a more direct path toward appointment enquiries.
